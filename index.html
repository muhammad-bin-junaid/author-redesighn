Meet the author

ABOUT AYESHA

Ayesha Marfani creates stories where imagination meets meaning. Through whimsical adventures, memorable characters, and heartfelt lessons, her books help young readers explore kindness, courage, identity, friendship, and the many feelings that shape growing up.

Beyond storytelling, Ayesha is an educator and wordscather who believes creativity should feel like discovery rather than homework. Her creative writing resources encourage children to find the characters hiding in their thoughts, explore new ideas, and turn their imagination into stories of their own.

She is also the founder of the Virtual Writing School, a learning space designed to help young people strengthen their writing, communication, and academic skills while building confidence in their own voice.
